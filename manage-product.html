👗 Fashion Details

These details are specifically for clothing and fashion products. They help customers choose the correct size, colour and fit, and they also support Sew on Demand / Made to Measure.

Choose the garment type so the correct measurement fields can appear automatically.

Size Chart Image

Optional. Upload a designed size or measurement chart image for customers.

Body Size Guide

These are the customer's BODY measurements for normal ready-to-wear sizing. The fields change according to the Garment Type above. Leave measurements that do not apply blank.

Garment Measurements

These are the measurements of the finished clothing itself. The fields change according to the Garment Type above and can differ from body measurements.

Sizes, Colours & Stock

Create the ready-to-wear combinations customers can buy. For example: Black / M / 4 in stock.

Stock can be controlled separately for each size and colour. Example: Black–M can have 4 pieces while Black–L has 2 pieces.

✂️ Sew on Demand / Made to Measure

Turn this on when customers can order this design sewn specially to their own measurements. Customer measurements will later be collected privately with the order, not displayed publicly.

Measurements Customer Will Be Asked For

These are selected automatically from the Garment Type. You can untick any measurement that is not needed for this particular design, for example Sleeve Length on a sleeveless gown.

Custom Measurements

If this design needs a special measurement not in the normal list, add it here. Example: Cape Length, Shoulder-to-Floor, Peplum Length.

Half Length and Under Bust are separate measurements. Garment-specific measurements remain different for Blouses/Tops, Skirts, Trousers, Gowns/Dresses, Jumpsuits and Sets.
