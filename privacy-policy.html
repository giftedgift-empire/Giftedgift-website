Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GiftedGift Empire collects, uses, stores and protects personal information, including our use of cookies, analytics and optional advertising technologies.

Last updated: 27 August 2026
← Back to Home
GiftedGift Empire respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly, transparently and in accordance with applicable data-protection laws.

1. Who We Are

GiftedGift Empire operates the website giftedgiftempire.com and provides physical products, digital products, African foodstuffs, affiliate recommendations, deals, content and related services.

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact:

Email: gift@giftedgiftempire.com

2. Information We May Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use GiftedGift Empire.

  • Contact information: such as your name, email address and telephone number.
  • Order and delivery information: such as delivery address, products ordered, quantities, shipping option and order status.
  • Payment-related information: such as payment status and transaction references. Payment-card information is handled by the relevant payment provider and is not intended to be stored directly by GiftedGift Empire.
  • Reviews and comments: such as your name, rating, review, Daily Inspiration comment and date of submission.
  • Messages and enquiries: information you provide when contacting us by email, WhatsApp or another communication channel.
  • Technical and browser information: information necessary for website operation, security and functionality, including browser type, device information, browser storage, cart information and similar technical data.
  • Optional analytics and advertising information: where you give the relevant consent, information about visits, pages viewed, traffic sources, interactions and advertising measurement may be processed through the technologies explained below.

3. How We Use Your Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • process and manage purchases;
  • arrange shipping and delivery;
  • provide digital products and downloads;
  • respond to customer-support questions;
  • communicate about an order or enquiry;
  • manage customer reviews and comments;
  • prevent misuse, fraud and security problems;
  • operate, maintain and improve the website;
  • measure website performance where analytics consent has been given;
  • measure advertising effectiveness where marketing consent has been given;
  • comply with accounting, tax, consumer-protection and other legal obligations.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable under data-protection law, we process personal information on one or more of the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract — for example, processing an order, payment, digital download or delivery.
  • Legal obligations — where information must be retained or processed to comply with applicable law.
  • Legitimate interests — such as website security, fraud prevention, customer service, moderation and improving our services, where those interests are not overridden by applicable rights.
  • Consent — where consent is legally required, including optional analytics, advertising cookies, pixels, tags and similar tracking technologies.

5. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as PayPal.

When you use a third-party payment service, that provider may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy and legal obligations.

GiftedGift Empire may receive payment confirmation, transaction identifiers and information necessary to complete or verify your order.

6. Shipping and Delivery

When physical delivery is required, necessary customer and delivery information may be shared with shipping or logistics providers for the purpose of fulfilling the order.

Only information reasonably necessary for delivery, tracking, customer service and related purposes should be provided to those service providers.

7. African Foodstuffs and WhatsApp

Some African Foodstuffs orders or enquiries may be submitted through WhatsApp.

When you choose to communicate through WhatsApp, your information is also processed by WhatsApp and its service providers according to their own terms and privacy practices.

8. Affiliate Links

Some links on GiftedGift Empire are affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link, you may be redirected to a third-party merchant or affiliate network.

Those third parties may collect information about your visit or purchase according to their own privacy and cookie policies.

GiftedGift Empire may receive a commission from qualifying purchases made through certain affiliate links, at no additional cost to you.

9. Reviews and Public Comments

If you submit a product review or Daily Inspiration comment, it may first be held for moderation.

When approved, the name you provided together with your review, rating or comment may become publicly visible on the website.

Do not include private, confidential or sensitive personal information in a public review or comment.

10. Cookies and Browser Storage

GiftedGift Empire uses browser-storage technologies for essential website functions and may use optional analytics or marketing technologies only according to the visitor's privacy choices.

Essential storage may be used for functions such as:

  • remembering the contents of your shopping cart;
  • remembering website or interface preferences;
  • supporting essential security or authentication;
  • remembering session-based website features;
  • remembering whether a welcome message has already been displayed during a browser session;
  • storing your cookie and privacy choices.

Optional analytics and marketing technologies are described in the dedicated Cookie & Tracking Technologies section below.

11. Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers to operate parts of GiftedGift Empire, including website hosting, database services, payment processing, delivery, communications, analytics, advertising measurement and technical services.

These providers may process personal information where necessary for the services they provide and may be subject to their own legal, contractual and privacy obligations.

12. International Data Transfers

Some service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence or outside the European Economic Area.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate legal safeguards are used for international transfers of personal data.

13. How Long We Keep Information

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and to meet applicable legal, accounting, tax, dispute-resolution and security requirements.

Different categories of information may therefore have different retention periods.

Cookie and privacy choices made through our consent interface are currently remembered in the visitor's browser for approximately 180 days, unless the choice expires, is reset, browser storage is cleared or the consent system is materially updated.

14. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to help protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

However, no website, internet connection or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Your Data-Protection Rights

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may have rights including:

  • the right to access personal information held about you;
  • the right to correct inaccurate information;
  • the right to request deletion of information;
  • the right to request restriction of processing;
  • the right to object to certain processing;
  • the right to data portability where applicable;
  • the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority.

In France, the relevant supervisory authority is the Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés (CNIL).

16. Children's Privacy

GiftedGift Empire is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children where parental or guardian consent would be legally required.

If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.

17. Third-Party Websites

GiftedGift Empire may contain links to websites operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites. Please review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our services, technology, business practices, tracking technologies or legal obligations change.

Where a material change affects optional analytics or marketing technologies, visitors may be asked to make their privacy choices again.

The current version will be published on this page together with its latest update date.

Cookie & Tracking Technologies

GiftedGift Empire separates optional technologies into Analytics and Marketing categories. Optional analytics or advertising technologies are not activated through our website unless the visitor gives the relevant permission.
Essential — Always Active

Essential Browser Storage

Essential browser storage supports functions requested by the visitor, such as the shopping cart, website preferences, privacy choices, session-based features and certain security or authentication functions.

These technologies are not used by GiftedGift Empire for optional advertising tracking.

Analytics Consent Required

Google Analytics 4

GiftedGift Empire uses Google Analytics 4 to help understand how visitors use the website.

Depending on the visitor's device and browser, analytics information may include pages visited, approximate traffic source, browser or device information, engagement information and website events.

Google Analytics is activated through GiftedGift Empire only after the visitor allows Analytics technologies.

Google may process information according to its own privacy terms and applicable data-protection obligations.

Marketing Consent Required

Meta / Facebook Pixel

GiftedGift Empire uses the Meta Pixel to measure activity associated with Facebook and Instagram advertising and to understand how visitors interact with the website after engaging with advertising.

Depending on the features used, Meta may receive information relating to website visits, browser or device information and events such as page views or, when implemented, ecommerce actions.

The Meta Pixel is activated through GiftedGift Empire only after the visitor allows Marketing technologies.

Meta processes information according to its own privacy terms and applicable data-protection obligations.

Marketing Consent Required

Pinterest Tag

GiftedGift Empire uses the Pinterest Tag to measure visits and actions associated with Pinterest traffic or advertising, understand campaign performance and support conversion measurement.

Depending on the visitor's device and the events configured on the website, Pinterest may receive information relating to website visits, technical information and website interactions.

The Pinterest Tag is activated through GiftedGift Empire only after the visitor allows Marketing technologies.

Pinterest processes information according to its own privacy terms and applicable data-protection obligations.

Marketing Consent Required

TikTok Pixel

GiftedGift Empire uses the TikTok Pixel for advertising measurement and to understand website activity associated with TikTok campaigns or traffic.

Depending on the events configured, TikTok may receive information relating to page visits, technical browser or device information and website interactions.

The TikTok Pixel is activated through GiftedGift Empire only after the visitor allows Marketing technologies.

TikTok processes information according to its own privacy terms and applicable data-protection obligations.

Not Currently Active

Google Ads / YouTube Advertising

At the date of this Privacy Policy, GiftedGift Empire has not activated Google Ads or YouTube advertising conversion tracking.

If these advertising technologies are introduced later, they will be treated as Marketing technologies and will not be activated through our website before the required Marketing consent is given.

Managing Your Privacy Choices

When a visitor first arrives, GiftedGift Empire provides controls for accepting optional technologies, rejecting optional technologies or managing Analytics and Marketing choices separately.

Rejecting optional technologies does not disable essential website functions such as normal browsing or the shopping cart.

The selected privacy choice is currently remembered in the browser for approximately 180 days.

Visitors can change or withdraw their choice at any time using the Cookie Settings control shown on the website.
