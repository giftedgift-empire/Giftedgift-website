Advertising With GiftedGift Empire

We provide selected advertisers with promotional space designed to help visitors discover relevant businesses, products, services, events and offers.

📢

Sponsored Promotion

Approved campaigns can appear as clearly labelled sponsored advertisements on GiftedGift Empire.

🖼️

Homepage Advertising

Homepage banner advertising may be available depending on campaign suitability, availability and approval.

📊

Campaign Tracking

Where supported, GiftedGift Empire can record advertisement impressions and clicks to help monitor campaign activity.

What Can You Advertise?

Applications are welcome from legitimate businesses and creators with suitable products, services and campaigns.

🛍️

Products & Brands

Promote legitimate physical products, online stores, brands and selected offers.

💻

Digital Products & Services

Promote suitable digital resources, professional services, courses, websites and online businesses.

📅

Events & Campaigns

Suitable events, launches, promotions and special campaigns may also be considered.

How It Works

Advertising is reviewed before anything is published on GiftedGift Empire.

1

Apply

Tell us about your business, advertisement and campaign.

2

Review

GiftedGift Empire reviews the application and destination.

3

Agree Campaign

If approved, placement, duration and applicable price are agreed before publishing.

4

Go Live

The approved advertisement is published for the agreed campaign period.

Advertising Standards

Protecting visitors and the reputation of GiftedGift Empire comes first.

Important

  • Every advertisement is subject to review and approval.
  • The advertiser must have the right to promote the submitted product, service, brand, image and content.
  • Advertising claims must be truthful and must not deliberately mislead visitors.
  • Illegal, fraudulent, harmful, deceptive or prohibited content will not be accepted.
  • GiftedGift Empire may reject or remove advertising that violates its policies or presents a risk to visitors.
  • Advertising does not guarantee clicks, customers, sales or revenue.
  • Pricing depends on the campaign, placement and duration and will be confirmed before an approved campaign goes live.
