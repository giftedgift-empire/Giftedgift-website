Creator Seller Agreement

By applying to become a Creator, you confirm that you understand the basic selling rules of GiftedGift Empire.

  • You must own or have the legal right to sell every digital product you submit.
  • Pirated, stolen or illegally copied products are prohibited.
  • PLR or licensed content must not be falsely presented as your original work.
  • Products making deceptive, illegal, fraudulent, medical or financial claims may be rejected or removed.
  • Every product must be approved by GiftedGift Empire before it becomes available for sale.
  • The default sale split is 70% to the Creator and 30% to GiftedGift Empire, unless another rate has been specifically agreed.
  • Creator withdrawals have a minimum threshold of €20.
  • Refunds, reversals and chargebacks may affect Creator earnings where applicable.
