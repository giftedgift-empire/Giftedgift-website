About GiftedGift Empire

An online shopping and digital commerce platform created to help people discover useful products, resources, opportunities and carefully selected recommendations.

What Is GiftedGift Empire?

GiftedGift Empire is an online shopping and digital commerce platform that brings together fashion and accessories, useful lifestyle products, health and wellness finds, digital products and resources, African foodstuffs, deals and carefully selected affiliate recommendations.

Our platform is designed to make it easier for customers to discover useful products and resources in one place. Some products are offered directly through GiftedGift Empire, some are sold by approved Creators through our Creator Marketplace, and some are recommendations from selected brands, merchants and affiliate partners.

GiftedGift Empire is also growing beyond shopping. We provide opportunities for approved Creators to sell eligible digital products, affiliates to promote qualifying products and earn commissions, and businesses to advertise their products, services, brands and events through our platform.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make online discovery simpler by bringing useful products, digital resources, African foodstuffs, fashion, lifestyle finds and selected recommendations together in one growing platform.

We aim to build a trusted environment where customers can explore products and resources, Creators can grow through digital selling, affiliates can earn from genuine referrals, and businesses can reach new audiences through advertising opportunities.

Some links on GiftedGift Empire may be affiliate links. When a qualifying purchase is made through certain links, GiftedGift Empire may earn a commission at no additional cost to the customer.
