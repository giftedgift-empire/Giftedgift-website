God's Word for Today

Cast Your Cares on God

“Cast your cares on the Lord and He will sustain you.”
Psalm 55:22
Whatever is weighing on your heart today, you do not have to carry it alone. Give your worries, fears and uncertainties to God and trust Him to strengthen you.
Today's Prayer:

Lord, I place every worry and burden in Your hands. Give me peace, strength and wisdom for today. Guide my steps and help me trust You completely. Amen.
