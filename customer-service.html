Frequently Asked Questions

These answers cover some common GiftedGift Empire support issues.

I have a problem with an order. What should I include?

Include your order or reference number, the email used for the purchase and a clear description of the issue.

I paid but did not receive my digital product.

Select Digital Download and include the payment or order reference. Do not submit card security details or passwords.

How do I request a refund?

Select Refund / Return and include the relevant order number and reason for your request. Refund eligibility depends on the applicable product and refund terms.

I cannot access my account.

Select Account / Login and explain what happens when you try to sign in. Never send your password through the support form.

I am a Creator and need help.

Select Creator / Seller Support and include your Seller Code when relevant.

I am an Affiliate and need help with earnings or payouts.

Select Affiliate Support and include your referral code, payout reference or other relevant information when available.

My enquiry is not listed. What should I choose?

Choose Something else. A small box will appear where you can briefly tell GiftedGift Empire what your enquiry is about.
